\* Working towards a sustainable future through advancing science and technology is my mission. In the long term, we need to make life multiplanetary. I hope humanity will have become a multiplanetary species by the time Earth faces a doomsday event in the future. As Konstantin Tsiolkovsky said, "The Earth is the cradle of humanity, but mankind cannot stay in the cradle forever."

\* Fostering equality of opportunity in our time even when technology evolves rapidly is important. The benefits of cutting-edge technology should be democratized throughout society so that our society does not become more polarized and no few exclusive groups de facto monopolize and control our society.

\* Sense of urgency, willfulness, and adaptability are key qualities in getting great things done. You should really believe in what you are working on; it is your mission to solve the problem and it also really matters to the world. If you believe what you are doing is correct, you should never give up. That being said, circumstances change, and often times our beliefs could be wrong. We need to be open to learning something new and recalibrate our directions quickly.

\* Delivering selfless service to others is the core philosophy of my life. At the end of the day, the purpose of my life is to serve others selflessly and make the world a better place to live in. I can never overstate the importance of reminding myself of that constantly and not letting greed and personal gains cloud my judgement.
